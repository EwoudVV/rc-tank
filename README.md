# rc-tank

## credits

The wheels, suspension arms, drive wheel, and tracks aren't my designs. They come from fastmike75's tank parts on Thingiverse, scaled up to about 206%.

- [Tank, Sprockets, Mag Idler Wheels, Suspension Arms](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2699624) by fastmike75, CC BY-NC
  - `design files/wheel1.stl` to `wheel4.stl`
  - `design files/i_susp_bar.stl` and `l_susp_bar.stl`
- [Tank Track 2in, Track, grousers, sprockets](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:471357) by fastmike75, CC BY-SA
  - `design files/tread.stl` and `grouser.stl`
  - `design files/drivewheel.stl`, which started as his drive sprocket B. I added a hub for a flange coupler.
  - His track is a remix of [Tank tracks, Flat surface, Heavy-Duty](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:163486) by night--wolf, CC BY-SA

The full tank exports in `design files/reference/` include these parts too.
